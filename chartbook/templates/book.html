How to read this book

This book was written from one thing only: the position of the Sun, Moon and planets at the moment you were born, seen from {{ birth_place }}. Every section ends with a small line, marked ✦, naming the placements it is based on, so you can always see why something was said about you.

Astrology is best used as a mirror, not a forecast. Some passages will feel uncannily familiar and others less so. Keep what rings true, question what doesn't, and notice which parts you find yourself thinking about later.

Signs
The twelve sections of the zodiac. A planet's sign describes the style in which that part of you operates.

Houses
Twelve areas of life, from self-image (1st) to the inner life (12th). A planet's house shows where its energy shows up most.
Rising sign
The sign that was coming up on the eastern horizon when you were born. It colors first impressions and how you meet the world.

Aspects
Angles between planets. Trines and sextiles flow easily; squares and oppositions create tension that pushes you to grow; conjunctions fuse two planets together.
Retrograde (℞)
A planet that appeared to move backwards from Earth when you were born, often read as energy that turns inward first.

A note before you go

A birth chart is a snapshot of a single moment, and you have been changing ever since. The patterns described here are tendencies, not instructions. You get to decide what to do with them.

If you come back to this book in a year, different chapters may stand out. That is part of the point.
