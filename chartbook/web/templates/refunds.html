Right of withdrawal (consumers in the EU and UK)

If you are a consumer living in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the law gives you a right to withdraw from a contract made online within 14 days without giving any reason. For digital content like our books, this right works as follows.

Withdrawal period

The withdrawal period is 14 days from the day the contract is made.

When the right ends early

Your book is made and delivered straight after payment. At checkout, we ask you to agree that we start immediately and to confirm that you understand that you lose your legal right of withdrawal once the book has been made available to you to download. We record when you agreed and confirm it on your receipt. Our 14-day refund promise above still applies after that.

How to withdraw

To withdraw, tell us clearly that you have decided to do so, before the book is made available: by email to {{ support_email }} or by post to {{ legal.name }}, {{ legal.address }}. You can use the model form below, but you don't have to.

Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw, we refund all payments we received from you without undue delay and no later than 14 days after we receive your notice, using the same payment method you used, unless we agree otherwise. You pay no fees for the refund.
