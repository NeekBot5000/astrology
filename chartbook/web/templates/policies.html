Refunds

What you pay

Privacy

To make a book we need a first name, birth date, birth time (optional) and birth place. We look up the place's coordinates and time zone using OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service. The chart facts (not your email) are sent to our AI writing provider, Anthropic, to write the text.

We keep these details only to make and deliver the book. They are erased {{ keep_days }} days after your book is ready, 2 days after a preview if you don't buy, or 120 days after you reserve a copy. You can erase them sooner from your download page, or by emailing us.

If you arrive through a tagged link (for example from one of our videos), we set one small cookie called src that remembers which link it was, for 30 days, so we know which posts are working. It contains only that tag. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we count page visits without identifying you.

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store card details. If you give an email address, we use it only for your receipt and your book, never for marketing unless you separately agree.

What the book is

The book is for entertainment and self-reflection. It is not a prediction and not professional advice of any kind. Planet positions are calculated with astronomical software; the text is written by an AI model from those positions and checked automatically.
