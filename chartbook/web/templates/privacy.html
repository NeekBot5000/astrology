3. Cookies

We don't use cookies, advertising trackers or third-party analytics on our website. Stripe's payment page may set its own cookies to process the payment and prevent fraud; see Stripe's privacy policy. Because we set no cookies that need consent, we don't show a cookie banner.

4. Who we share data with

We use a few service providers ("processors") who handle data on our behalf, under contracts that oblige them to protect it:

  • Stripe (payments): your email, payment details and the order amount. Stripe is also an independent controller for fraud prevention and its own legal duties.
  • Anthropic (AI writing): the calculated chart (planet positions and the first name). Not your email, and not your exact birth place. Data sent through Anthropic's commercial API is not used to train its models.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim place search): the place name you typed, to find its coordinates.
  • [Your hosting provider, e.g. Render]: stores the order database and book files on our behalf.

Some of these providers are in the United States. Where data leaves the EU or UK, it is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.
