Questions

Good to know

Who writes the book?

The planet positions are calculated by astronomical software. The text is written by an AI model that sees only those calculated placements, under strict rules: every paragraph must be based on specific placements, which we check automatically before the book is made. That's why each section can show you exactly where it comes from.

What if I don't know the birth time?

You'll still get a full book. We use midday, leave out the rising sign and houses (they change every few minutes), and say so clearly in the book.

Is it a good gift?

That's what it's designed for. Add a note for the first page, then download the PDF to email or print. It's sized like a paperback (6 × 9 inches) and prints well at home or at a copy shop.

How is this different from a free horoscope or chatbot?

Horoscopes use only the Sun sign, which you share with one in twelve people. This book uses the whole chart: every planet, its house and the angles between them, calculated for the exact place and time. And it shows its working on every page.

Do you keep my information?

Only as long as needed. Birth details and the book file are erased {{ keep_days }} days after delivery, and you can erase them immediately from your download page. Card details go straight to our payment provider; we never see or store them.

Is astrology scientifically proven?

No. We think of a birth chart as a mirror for reflection, not a prediction. The book says so too, and avoids predictions about health, money or relationships.
