# Founder forecast (2026-10-13, before building the product)

This is a judgment call based on the brief's market numbers and on what milestone 1 turned up. It is not a guarantee. Most of what decides the outcome (whether strangers pay, which posts take off) can only be learned by testing.

## Bottom line
Building the product is the easy part. Milestone 1 took one session, and the full PDF generator is probably 2-4 more. The real risk is getting strangers to find it and pay. The most likely future is a nice product that earns a little. You have a reasonable shot at a small side income if one content channel works.

| Outcome 12 months after launch | Brief's estimate | My estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Under $1,000/month | ~60% | ~65% |
| $1,000-$10,000/month | ~30% | ~28% |
| Over $10,000/month | 5-10% | ~7% |

I'm slightly more pessimistic than the brief because one competitor already sells this exact idea ("personalized book + companion app") and has hundreds of reviews. That doesn't kill the idea, but we have to win on quality and honesty, not on being new.

## Why it could work
- **Cheap to test.** A one-time $15-40 gift needs no subscription machinery, and you get a clear yes/no from a landing page within weeks.
- **Good margins.** Each book costs roughly $0.20-$1 in AI writing, plus about 3% + $0.30 in card fees. At $29 you keep about $27 before marketing.
- **Gift demand all year.** There are birthdays every day, plus peaks around Christmas, Valentine's Day and Mother's Day.
- **A real quality gap.** Competitor reviews complain about generic text. Our calculator already matches a published reference chart to the arcminute, and every paragraph can cite a real placement.

## Why it could fail (most likely first)
1. **Nobody finds it.** Competitors buy TikTok and Instagram ads. Paying $15-30+ in ads per sale would wipe out a $29 product, so free content has to carry it: posting sample readings and chart breakdowns on TikTok, Reddit and Pinterest.
2. **"ChatGPT does this for free."** If the sample chapter doesn't feel clearly more personal than a free chatbot answer, people won't pay. The sample chapter (milestone 3) is the make-or-break piece.
3. **A licensing surprise (new finding).** The Swiss Ephemeris, the planet-position engine we use, is free only under the AGPL license. For a website, that means publishing the whole site's source code. The alternative is Astrodienst's paid professional license (about CHF 750 one-time; check the current price). **You need to pick one before launch.** Publishing the code is a fine choice for a gift product: the code isn't the moat, the writing and the marketing are.
4. **Refunds and chargebacks** (a customer disputing the charge with their bank) on digital goods. Showing a real preview before checkout reduces both.

## What to measure (early warning signs)
- **Landing page:** at least 2 paid pre-orders per 100 interested visitors. Under 0.5 per 100 means rework the angle or the price.
- **Refunds:** under 5% of orders.
- **Shares and gifts:** if buyers send the book to someone else, that's free marketing. Track it from the start.

## Kill or pivot rule (concrete version of the brief's rule)
After about 1,000 visitors who were actually targeted (from your own posts, not bots), if there are fewer than 5 paid orders, stop or change the angle. Possible new angles: couples/compatibility books, a baby's first birth chart for new parents, or a printed hardcover partnership.

## Timeline prediction
- **Weeks 1-3:** chart wheel image, sample chapter, landing page with pre-order (milestones 2-4).
- **Weeks 3-6:** the pre-sale test. This is the moment of truth.
- **Month 2-3:** if it passes, full PDF and payments. If it fails, pivot or stop, having spent little.
- **Month 6-12:** add the daily-readings subscription only if one-time sales prove demand.
